Reference sidebars

Use PREVENT for 10-year total CVD risk in eligible adults. Do not insert Lp(a) into PREVENT. Calculator exclusions (<1 year life expectancy, etc.) differ from CPG population exclusions.

CAC (Recs 3–4), Lp(a) once lifetime (Rec 5), and insufficient evidence for ABI, ApoB, PRS, TPA, hs-CRP (Rec 6). Imaging-only atherosclerosis does not independently define secondary prevention (see Sidebar 3).

Clinical ASCVD for secondary prevention excludes asymptomatic imaging-only atherosclerosis. Use established clinical ASCVD diagnoses for Box 5.

All three very-high-risk criteria require current lipid-lowering therapy. ASCVD with LDL-C ≥70 mg/dL without therapy is not automatically very high risk.

Moderate- and high-intensity statin definitions as in the CPG. Outputs are intensity guidance and option sets, not prescriptions.

Ezetimibe, PCSK9 monoclonal antibodies, and bempedoic acid appear in secondary and statin-intolerance option sets (Recs 13–14, 19). Unranked when the CPG presents alternatives.

Rechallenge before switching (Rec 18). Unable-to-take-statin options (Rec 19). Intolerance and inability are clinician-confirmed.

Secondary-prevention IPE when statin use and persistently elevated fasting triglycerides ≥150 mg/dL are established (Rec 16). Suggest against non-IPE omega-3 supplements (Rec 21). Triglycerides >500 mg/dL relate to genetic-dyslipidemia population exclusion.

Agents surfaced by recommendations include statins (intensity per Sidebar 5), ezetimibe, PCSK9 monoclonal antibodies, bempedoic acid, fibrates (suggest against adding to statin — Rec 12), and icosapent ethyl. This panel is a reference aid only; it does not authorize or rank orders.

Disclaimer

The VA/DoD CPG is not intended to define a standard of care or exclusive course of management. This application provides clinical decision support only. Medication outputs are option sets for shared decision-making, never prescriptions or orders. Source: Lipids CPG final PDF dated 2026-01-06 (guideline December 2025), including Appendix G (pp. 125–127) and Table 4 Recommendations 1–24 (pp. 24–26). Distinctions preserved: Box 14 practice point vs Rec 2; Box 15 reassessment vs Rec 15 (LDL-C <30); Box 20 dual monitoring vs Rec 17 neither; Box 6 six-week cue vs Rec 24 recent CHD rehab.
