Interpretation

How to read and apply results from the OpenCVDRisk Calculator.

Intended use

The calculator estimates 10- and 30-year total cardiovascular disease (CVD) risk using an open-source implementation of the American Heart Association (AHA) PREVENT™ simplified equations. Results are clinical decision support for shared decision-making—not treatment orders, diagnoses, or a substitute for clinician judgment.

Who it applies to

Scoring is intended for adults ages 30–79 without known CVD and without PREVENT exclusions (for example limited life expectancy or a pathogenic genetic CVD variant). When chart or clinician-attested exclusions are present, the calculator warns that equations may not apply; you may still compute risk after acknowledging that limitation.

Limitations

  • Not an official AHA product and not affiliated with or endorsed by the AHA. Model coefficients follow published PREVENT simplified regression (Khan et al.).
  • Estimates depend on accurate inputs (lipids, blood pressure, kidney function, smoking, medications, and optional UACR, HbA1c, or social deprivation index). Missing or out-of-range values can change model selection or block calculation.
  • Risk is a population-derived probability, not a guarantee of an individual outcome. Use alongside clinical context, patient preferences, and applicable guidelines (including lipid management guidelines where relevant).
  • Chart prefill reflects available FHIR data and may be incomplete or stale; review and correct values before relying on results.

Reading results

Report absolute 10- and 30-year risk percentages (and related outputs such as PREVENT-Risk Age when shown) as decision-support signals.
